**Q:** What is Sol Invictus?  
**A:** Sol Invictus is a collaborative solar engineering initiative competing in the Sasol Solar Challenge 2026. The project brings together students, academics and industry partners to design, build and optimise a high-performance solar vehicle while developing future-ready skills.

**Q:** Why is Belgium Campus iTversity involved in the project?  
**A:** Belgium Campus iTversity believes students learn best by applying their knowledge to real-world challenges. Sol Invictus provides an opportunity for students to gain practical experience in technology, innovation, and collaborative problem-solving.

**Q:** How does Sol Invictus support Learning 2.0?  
**A:** The project reflects the Learning 2.0 philosophy by allowing students to learn through hands-on experience, industry collaboration and multidisciplinary teamwork while contributing to a live engineering project.

**Q:** Which technologies do students work with?  
**A:** Students gain exposure to a range of industry-relevant technologies, including Artificial Intelligence (AI), Software Development, Data Analytics, IoT, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Embedded Systems and Communications Technologies.

**Q:** What skills do students gain through the project?  
**A:** Students develop practical technical skills, project management experience, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving abilities, and exposure to professional industry workflows.

**Q:** Which institutions participate in Sol Invictus?  
**A:** The project is a collaboration between Belgium Campus iTversity, IMM Graduate School, Open Window Institute and CTU Training Solutions, supported by industry partners and mentors.

**Q:** Do students from different disciplines collaborate on the project?  
**A:** Yes. Sol Invictus brings together students with expertise in technology, engineering, design, communication, and innovation, creating a multidisciplinary environment that mirrors modern industry.

**Q:** How does the project prepare students for industry?  
**A:** By working on a real-world project with measurable outcomes, students gain practical experience, build professional networks, strengthen their portfolios, and develop skills that are highly valued by employers.

**Q:** Why is the Sasol Solar Challenge important?  
**A:** The Sasol Solar Challenge provides a platform for innovation, sustainable engineering, and practical problem-solving. It allows students to evaluate technologies and ideas in a challenging real-world environment.

**Q:** How can I support or get involved with Sol Invictus?  
**A:** There are several ways to become part of the journey, whether as a student, industry partner, sponsor, or supporter. You can follow the team’s progress, connect with the partners involved, or explore opportunities to collaborate with the project.
