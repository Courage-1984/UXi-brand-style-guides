Sol Invictus is a multidisciplinary innovation project that brings together students, academics, and industry partners to develop and optimise a high-performance solar-electric vehicle for the Sasol Solar Challenge 2026. Through the project, Belgium Campus iTversity students apply their knowledge in a live engineering environment while gaining valuable industry experience.

H2: Developing Emerging Technologies

H3: From Data to Performance

The project creates opportunities for students to contribute to software systems, telemetry solutions, data visualisation tools and intelligent technologies that support vehicle performance and decision-making. Every challenge becomes an opportunity to innovate, test and improve.

A recent milestone included the development of an AI-enabled telemetry and neural network solution that allows the solar vehicle and support vehicle to communicate in real time, supporting race strategy, efficiency and safety.

Outcome: Experience building technology that solves real-world engineering challenges.
