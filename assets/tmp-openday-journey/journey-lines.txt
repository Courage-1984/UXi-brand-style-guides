Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any coding or IT experience to attend?

No. Open Day is designed for anyone interested in technology, whether you're completely new to IT or already exploring it as a career option.

Can I attend the Open Day if I'm still in high school?

Yes. Open Day is ideal for learners who are considering their study options and want to learn more about careers in technology.

My child is interested in IT. Can I attend with them?

Yes. Parents are encouraged to attend so they can learn more about qualifications, career opportunities, admissions, and funding options.

What if I'm not sure which IT qualification is right for me?

That's exactly what Open Day is for. Our team can help you understand the different study paths and identify the qualification that best matches your interests and career goals.

Can I get help with applications?

Yes. Our admissions team can assist with your application and provide guidance on available funding options and processes. – I am a bit apprehensive to speak to funding, this is probably also why people think we are to an extent free.

What if I can't attend in person?

You can attend Open Day online and still explore your study options and engage with the Belgium Campus team.
